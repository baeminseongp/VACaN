# Repository scope

This is the Git-ready VACaN source distribution. It preserves the files needed
to reproduce the implemented Stage 0–7 pipeline and to build a visual analytics
client without publishing local runtimes. The complete retained Stage 7 Office
run archive is included as an explicit exception to the normal artifact policy.

## Included

- `src/vacan/`: simulator boundaries, mapping, recurrent PPO, logging, and
  supporting research code.
- `configs/`: versioned Isaac Sim, scenario, costmap, mapping, and PPO YAML.
- `scripts/`: host checks, Isaac Sim entry points, validators, and visualization
  utilities.
- `requirements/` and `pyproject.toml`: pinned environment metadata.
- `tests/`: lightweight unit and contract tests.
- `docs/`: architecture, third-party notes, and Stage 0–7 evidence reports.
- `checkpoints/`: the selected frozen Office Recurrent PPO policy and metadata,
  stored with Git LFS.
- `reproduction/`: the minimum Office static map and matched Stage 6 records
  needed for the Stage 7 host decision replay, stored with Git LFS where large.
- `examples/web-data/`: small browser-oriented samples from the current
  Office, Hospital, and Warehouse pipeline.
- `docs/media/`: compact trajectory/map images and one derived costmap replay,
  with source provenance documented alongside the files.
- Three tracked `artifacts/stage1-isaac/.../resolved_routes.json` files: the
  validated deterministic pedestrian routes referenced by Stage 2 and Stage 5.
- `artifacts/stage7/`: all six retained Office closed-loop runs and the
  standalone decision replay output (392 original files plus the archive
  README), with large data stored through Git LFS.

## Intentionally excluded

- `.venv/`, Python caches, test caches, local simulator installations, and
  externally downloaded Isaac Sim assets.
- Other `artifacts/` output, TensorBoard runs, periodic policy
  snapshots, and evaluation corpora. These are generated by the documented
  commands and are too large or machine-specific for normal Git.
- The retired ATC/CAVIAR/JRDB/Mall prototype and its static dashboard. Its data
  contracts do not describe the current Isaac Sim/Pegasus/Nova Carter pipeline.
- Secrets and local environment files such as `.env`.

Ground Truth remains separate from policy-observed state. The samples under
`examples/web-data` are presentation fixtures, not training input and not a
replacement for the simulator-generated acceptance evidence.

No Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, Pegasus, or NVIDIA asset is redistributed. The one
selected Office policy is redistributed solely for deterministic Stage 6–7
reproduction; its provenance and digest are recorded beside the file.
