# Quantitative evaluation summary

The early ATC system evaluation is documented in
[`docs/evaluation/step8-foundation.md`](../../docs/evaluation/step8-foundation.md).
It was implemented before roadmap reconciliation and is not the CAVIAR Step 6
validation. Regenerate its paper-facing tables and SVG figures with:

```bash
make atc-evaluation
```

Generated artifacts are copied to `figures/generated/atc-step8-foundation`; the complete
paired records and input-hash manifest remain in
`experiments/outputs/atc-step8-foundation`.
